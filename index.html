Security & control

AnoSphere is designed so users stay in control. Security features evolve over time — without changing the core principle.

Keys on your device

The wallet is designed to keep private keys on the user’s device (non-custodial). Consider enabling device passcode/biometrics for additional protection.

Confirmation guardrails

The app disables the send button during processing and can show a final confirmation dialog to help reduce accidental double sends.

Important note

Crypto assets can be risky. Always verify addresses, amounts, and network details before sending. If you lose access to your keys, you may lose access to funds.

Decentralization

Designed to interact with a decentralized network. Availability and performance can depend on network conditions.

What this app is (and isn’t)

The AnoSphere App is a client for a decentralized network. It does not accept deposits, hold funds, or run an exchange.

It is

• A non-custodial wallet for Anos
• A client for creating and using an AnoPod (your private, user-controlled storage space)
• A way to interact with network endpoints you choose

It is not

• A custodial wallet or hosted account
• A broker, exchange, or money transmitter
• A place where fiat money is loaded, stored, or sent

Security & control

The AnoSphere App is designed so users stay in control. Security features evolve over time — without changing the core principle.

Keys on your device

Private keys and encryption keys are generated on your device and stored using your device’s secure storage (e.g., OS keychain/keystore / secure enclave features, depending on platform).

Convenient Transactions

The app automatically calculates and sends the correct fee and other information to ensure smooth transactions.

Important note

Digital assets can be risky. Always verify addresses, amounts, and network details before sending. Transactions may be irreversible. If you lose access to your keys, you may lose access to funds, if your account has not been properly set up with the anoSphere network.

Proper Account Set up

The app helps to make sure you set your anoPod correctly in the anoSphere.

FAQ

Is this AnoSphere App custodial?

No. AnoSphere is intended to be non-custodial, meaning the user controls their private keys and funds are not held by us.

Is this investment advice?

No. Nothing on this site is financial or investment advice. Use at your own risk and do your own research.

When will the app be available?

Store listings are coming soon. This page will be updated with official links once published.

Who operates this app?

The app is published by anosphereApp LLC (D-U-N-S: 143351010).

Does the app buy/sell Anos or handle fiat money?

No. The app does not sell Anos, does not accept fiat deposits, and does not process payments. It only signs user-authorized requests and sends them to the decentralized network.

Is there a marketplace?

The anoShere App helps you to connect to your anoPod and the anoSphere which operates a distributed, non-custodial marketplace where users can discover other users to trade with. Trades happen peer-to-peer over that decentralized network. The publisher does not take custody of funds.

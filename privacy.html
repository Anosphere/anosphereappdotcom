Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Month Day, Year]

This Privacy Policy explains how the AnoSphere mobile application (“AnoSphere”, “App”) and this website handle information. AnoSphere is intended to be a non-custodial wallet: we do not hold your private keys or custody your funds.

Information we may collect

We aim to collect minimal information. Depending on how you use the App and which features you enable, we may collect:

• Support communications (emails you send to us)
• Basic diagnostics (crash logs / performance data) if enabled by your device or platform
• Device/app metadata (app version, OS version) for troubleshooting

Information we do not collect

We do not want or need your private keys. We do not intentionally collect:

• Your private keys or seed phrases
• Your PIN/biometric data (handled by your device)
• Your contacts
• Your precise location
• Your personal identity unless you choose to provide it in support messages

Non-custodial note

Because the App is non-custodial, you are responsible for safeguarding your device and keys. If you lose access to your keys, you may lose access to funds. We cannot recover private keys we do not possess.

How we use information

We use information only to:

• Provide support and respond to requests
• Improve app stability and fix bugs
• Protect against abuse and attacks on our services (where applicable)

Third-party services

The App may interact with third-party services (for example: app stores, hosting providers, or analytics/crash reporting if you enable them). Those services may collect information under their own policies. We recommend reviewing the relevant third-party privacy policies.

Data retention

We retain support communications and diagnostic data only as long as needed for support, security, and legal compliance.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by your local law). If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact:

privacy@anosphereapp.com

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above and post the revised policy on this page.
